Process

Three Steps to a Diagnosis

1

Upload Your Image

Take a photo of a tomato leaf and upload it. JPG, PNG, and WebP supported up to 16 MB.

2

AI Analyses the Leaf

The Custom CNN resizes your image to 64×64, runs inference, and computes probabilities for all four classes.

3

Get Your Diagnosis

Receive the predicted disease class, confidence score, probability breakdown, Grad-CAM heatmap, and treatment advice.

4

Take Action

Follow the guided recommendations — from fungicide application to plant isolation — to protect your crop.

Detection Targets

Four Tomato Leaf Conditions

The model is trained and validated on the PlantVillage dataset — 821 held-out test images.

✓

Healthy

No disease detected. Continue routine care and monitoring.

⚠

Early Blight

Alternaria solani. Concentric brown ring lesions on older leaves.

✕

Late Blight

Phytophthora infestans. Water-soaked dark patches. Urgent treatment required.

◈

Leaf Mold

Passalora fulva. Yellow patches above, grey-brown mould below leaf.
